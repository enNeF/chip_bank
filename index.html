    Tonight's results

      Net is chips now minus chips bought in. Chips sitting in the pot still count for whoever put them in. Use + Buy-in for rebuys so the numbers stay right.

      The goal

      Win chips. You win a hand in one of two ways: have the best five-card hand when the cards are shown, or bet so that everyone else folds.

      Before the cards

      One player has the dealer button (D). The player on their left pays the small blind and the next player pays the big blind. Blinds are forced bets so there is always something to win. The button moves one seat left every hand. Chip Bank does all of this for you.

      How a hand goes

      1. Deal

        Everyone gets 2 cards face down. These are your hole cards. Only you see them.

      2. Pre-flop betting

        Starts with the player left of the big blind. Each player folds, calls the big blind, or raises.

      3. The flop

        Dealer burns one card, then puts 3 cards face up in the middle. Everyone shares these. Betting starts left of the dealer.

      4. The turn

        Burn one, deal a 4th shared card. Another round of betting.

      5. The river

        Burn one, deal the 5th and last shared card. Final round of betting.

      6. Showdown

        Players still in show their cards. Make the best 5-card hand from your 2 cards plus the 5 on the table. Best hand wins the pot.

      Your options

      Check
      Pass without betting. Only allowed if nobody has bet this round.
      Bet
      Put chips in when nobody has bet yet.
      Call
      Match the current bet to stay in.
      Raise
      Increase the bet. A raise must be at least as big as the last bet or raise.
      Fold
      Throw your cards away and give up the pot. Chips you already put in stay there.
      All-in
      Bet every chip you have. You can only win from each player as much as you put in. The extra goes into a side pot.

      Using Chip Bank

      1. Set up

        Tap Setup to enter names in seat order, starting chips and blinds.

      2. Start the hand

        Blinds post automatically. The player with the gold outline acts next.

      3. Tap their action

        Type a raise amount or use Min, ½ pot or Pot. The app moves to the flop, turn and river when betting is done. Deal the real cards then.

      4. Pay the winner

        At showdown, tap the winning player and press Pay winner. Tie? Tap both to split. Not sure who won? Tap Check hands, enter the cards, and the app works it out.

      5. End of the night

        Open Results to see who is up or down. Busted players can rebuy from there or from their seat.

      Want blinds to go up over time? In Setup, set Blinds up every to 15 or 20 minutes. New blinds start from the next hand.

      Pressed the wrong button? Undo takes back one action at a time.

      Beginner tips

      • Play fewer hands. Strong starting cards like big pairs (A-A, K-K, Q-Q) and A-K win more often.
      • Acting last is an advantage because you see what others do first. The dealer seat acts last after the flop.
      • Watch the board for flushes and straights that someone else could have.
      • Don't keep calling just because you already put chips in.

      Settling ties

      • Card order, high to low: A K Q J 10 9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2.
      • Suits never break a tie. Two equal hands split the pot.
      • You always use your best 5 cards out of 7. You can use both, one or none of your own cards.
      • If the 5 table cards are the best hand for everyone, all remaining players split.
      Common mix-up: a flush beats a straight, and a full house beats a flush.
